Capture. Mark up. Hide what matters.

A free screenshot tool for the Mac menu bar. Drag over an area, draw on it, then copy, save or share it. Emails, card numbers and keys are covered before anyone else sees them.

Build it on your Mac See the code on GitHub

For Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 15 or newer. Everything runs on your Mac.

One shortcut to capture, one key for the rest

Snapline lives in the menu bar. Press the shortcut, drag over what you need, and finish with a single key. It works like Lightshot, rebuilt for Apple silicon.

⇧⌘1
Capture an area. The screen freezes; drag, move and resize your selection. You can change the shortcut.
⌘C
Copy the image, ready to paste anywhere.
⇧⌘C
Copy the text in it. Read on your Mac, in reading order. Keyboard symbols like ⌘ and ⇧ come through too.
⌘S
Save to your Desktop or any folder you pick in Preferences.
⌘D
Upload and copy the link. To your own S3-compatible bucket or your own Google Drive.
⌘E
Open in another app, such as Preview.
⌘P
Print it.
PART
Draw on it: pencil, arrow, rectangle, text, plus line, ellipse and marker.
H
Hide an area by hand when auto-hide missed something.

Sensitive data is covered before you share

When the screen freezes, Snapline reads it on your Mac and puts a solid box over anything that looks private. Click a box to show it again.

  • Email addresses and phone numbers
  • Card numbers and IBANs, checked so ordinary numbers stay visible
  • API keys, tokens, passwords and long random strings
  • Faces

Auto-hide is a safety net, not a guarantee. Very small, faint or rotated text can be missed, so check the image before you share it.

Nothing leaves your Mac unless you send it

Snapline has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no tracking. Capturing, auto-hide and Copy Text use Apple's built-in frameworks on your Mac.

  • Upload sends the image to the place you set up: your own bucket or your own Google Drive.
  • Search similar images uploads it there first, then opens Google Lens or TinEye with the link.
  • Email hands the file to your mail app.
  • Bucket keys and your Google sign-in are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Details, including what Snapline does with Google account data: Privacy policy.

Build it on your Mac

Snapline is built from source, which takes about a minute. You need a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 15 or newer, and Xcode.

  1. Get the code.

    git clone https://github.com/barayuda/Snapline.git
cd Snapline

  2. Build it and put it in Applications.

    scripts/make-app.sh --install

  3. Press ⇧⌘1. macOS asks once for Screen Recording permission; allow it, and you are set.
